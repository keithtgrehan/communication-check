# Human validation plan — not yet run

Keith recruits participants. No outreach has been sent, no participants are
claimed, and the app has no feedback collection or telemetry.

## Three usability sessions (15–20 minutes)

Ask for voluntary consent to observe task completion and take anonymous notes.
Use the three fictional demos, or a participant's invented/redacted draft. Do not
collect customer emails, confidential product commitments, recordings or names
without a separate explicit agreement. Participation can stop at any time.

Say: “This is a wording checker. It cannot verify facts or meeting agreements.
We are testing the product, not you. Please say what you expect each step to do.”

1. Load a fictional example and explain the purpose of the checker.
2. Run a check, explain one finding and find its exact wording in the editor.
3. Edit that wording; describe what happens to old results; recheck.
4. Dismiss a finding that does not help and explain why.
5. Copy the current draft, then clear it.
6. Explain whether any facts or agreements have been verified.

Record anonymous session ID, completion, assistance, misunderstood language,
useful/rejected prompt and severity of friction. Do not copy draft text into notes.
Desired signals: all three finish, at least two unaided, and no belief that facts
or agreements were verified. These are decision signals, not statistical claims.

## Five AM/CSM pilots over seven working days

Participants choose fictional or sufficiently redacted onboarding follow-ups.
Request voluntary reports of task completion, useful/rejected findings, return
use and a description of a useful revision without original customer text.
Nothing is sent automatically by the app; reports must use an agreed separate
channel and consent. Keith owns recruitment and collection.

Directional continuation signals: four participants complete two uses, three
return unprompted and three describe a useful revision. Do not mark unreported
uses as completed. Small, self-selected feedback cannot establish accuracy or
market fit. Record contradictions and reasons to stop or narrow scope.
