## LinkedIn draft — not posted

After an onboarding call, a follow-up can leave a customer unsure who acts next—or include a delivery promise that still needs confirmation.

Communication Check explores a short review step before sending. The writer sees an exact quote and a practical question, then decides whether to keep or edit the wording. Rechecking and copying complete the workflow; nothing is automatically rewritten or sent.

I set the product direction, audience, scope and presentation priorities and directed the AI-assisted build. Codex/Astra agents implemented the application and ran the documented engineering checks.

The React/TypeScript prototype checks drafts locally in the browser, with no runtime AI model. That keeps the behavior inspectable, but limits language coverage. All ten original reserved fictional evaluations missed the detection target. One expert reviewed example content; customer usability and repeat use remain unvalidated.

The case study explains the architecture, evidence and tradeoffs. The next question is whether real AMs and CSMs find the review useful enough to repeat.
